# Amazon_Sales_Dashboard 
Overview
This Amazon Sales Dashboard was developed in Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer engagement, and product demand trends using interactive KPI reporting and business intelligence visualizations. The dashboard tracks key business metrics including Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales, Quarter-to-Date (QTD) Revenue, Product Sales Volume, and Customer Review Activity to evaluate overall business performance. Through weekly and monthly trend analysis, the dashboard identifies peak sales periods, seasonal demand fluctuations, and revenue growth patterns across different product categories. Category-wise sales comparison helps determine the highest revenue-generating products and their contribution to total sales performance, while customer review metrics provide insights into customer engagement and high-demand products.
